## The change

Kea Web Creations stops selling *websites* and starts selling *a system that stops local
service businesses losing leads*. Every plan still starts with a website built for the
business, but the website is one part of a system that catches every enquiry, answers it
fast, follows it up, books the estimate, asks for the review and brings past customers back.

**Internal line only, never on the site:** "We stopped selling websites and started selling
customers." Kea cannot control demand, pricing, sales skill or seasonality, so the public
promise is about *not losing* customers who already found the business.

### Terms

- **Standard plans:** no contract, cancel any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of
  the current billing month.
- **Founding clients:** 90-day commitment, then month-to-month.
- Setup fees are non-refundable — they pay for work already done.
- **Updates:** 30 minutes of small updates a month (text, photos, hours, prices, phone
  numbers), no rollover. Extra time is $50/hour. New pages and features are quoted first.
- **Not included:** ad spend, photography, copywriting (quoted separately).
- **Texting and calling usage:** normal usage is included; unusually high volume is billed
  at cost.

### If you cancel

| Item | What happens |
|---|---|
| Domain, Google Business Profile, Google reviews | Always yours — they live in your accounts; Kea is only added as a manager |
| Website files and content | Handed over to you |
| Contacts and conversation history | Exported to you on request within 30 days |
| Business texting number | You can move it to another provider within 30 days; after that it is released |
| Workflows, automations and templates | Stay with Kea — they are part of the Kea system |

   website built for your business. The difference is how much of the follow-up runs for you."
2. **Founding clients banner** — founding offer and spots remaining.
3. **Plan cards** (`#capture`, `#convert`, `#keep`) — outcome headline, standard setup,
   founding setup, monthly, full inclusion list. Button → `contact.html?plan=capture` etc.
4. **Website only card.**
5. **Terms strip** — No contract on standard plans · Setup non-refundable · 30 min/month of
   updates · Your domain, website and contacts stay yours.
6. **Build your own plan** (`#build`) — rows from the table above; Google Ads row disabled;
   live setup and monthly totals; plan nudge; "Send me this plan" → `contact.html` with
   `?plan=custom&services=<ids>`.
7. **Lead leak calculator** (`#calculator`)
   - Inputs, each a slider with a number box: enquiries per month (example 40), share
     missed or answered too slowly (example 25%), share of answered enquiries that become
     jobs (example 35%), average job value (example $450).
   - Output: estimated lost revenue per month and per year, and "jobs lost per month".
     Then one line per plan: "Capture is $250/mo — about N job(s) a month covers it."
   - Formula printed beneath: enquiries × missed share × close rate × job value.
   - Labelled: "Example numbers — change them to yours. This is an estimate, not a promise."
8. **Pricing FAQ** (`#faq`) — what setup covers · founding vs standard · texting and calling
   usage · how long setup takes (texting registration takes days to weeks) · the 30-minute
   update allowance · what happens if you cancel (links to the table in `policy.html`) ·
   does Local SEO guarantee rankings.
9. **Closing CTA** — free lead-leak audit.

**Single source of truth:** `pricing.js` holds `SERVICES` (id, name, description, icon,
setup, monthly, comingSoon) and `PLANS` (id, name, standardSetup, foundingSetup, monthly,
serviceIds). Builder rows, totals, nudge and calculator plan lines all read from these.
Plan cards in HTML carry prices as static text so they index without JavaScript; a build-
time check is not available, so the verification step compares them to `PLANS` by hand.

## Contact page (`contact.html`) — audit request

- Heading: "Get a free lead-leak audit." Sub: what the audit covers (as homepage §8) and
  "I reply within one business day."
- Fields: name*, email*, phone, business name, trade (HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical ·
  Roofing · Landscaping · Other), website, interest (Free audit · Capture · Convert · Keep ·
  Website only · Build my own plan · Not sure yet), message, and an optional unticked
  texting-consent checkbox with the consent wording from `policy.html`.
- `?plan=` pre-selects interest; `?services=` writes the chosen services into the message.
- Submits to the existing `keaInquiry` function. It accepts only `name` (200), `email` (320),
  `phone` (50), `website` (300), `interest` (200) and `message` (5000) and ignores other keys,
  so business name, trade, chosen services and consent are prepended to `message` as labelled
  lines. No backend change.
- Keeps the existing honeypot and three-second fill-time check.
- Shows Laakea's phone number and email beside the form.
