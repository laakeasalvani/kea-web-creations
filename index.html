Our Policy

How projects run, what's expected on both sides, and what happens with your information.

Project terms

Work begins once a proposal is signed and a deposit is received. The balance is due at launch. Scope is fixed in writing; anything added mid-project is quoted separately before it's started, never after.

Revisions

Two rounds are included at each stage. Beyond that, additional rounds are billed at the hourly rate quoted in your proposal. Consolidated feedback in one pass keeps projects on schedule and costs down.

Ownership

On final payment, all rights to the delivered work transfer to you, including source files. Third-party assets — fonts, stock imagery, plugins — remain under their own licenses, listed in your handoff document.

Privacy

Information submitted through this site is used only to respond to your enquiry. It isn't sold, rented or shared with third parties. Ask at any time and it's deleted.

Cancellation

Either side can end a project in writing. You're billed for work completed to that point and receive every file produced so far. Deposits cover work already begun and aren't refundable.
